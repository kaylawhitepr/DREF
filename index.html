01 / A personal note

Scholarship.
Sisterhood.
Service.
Social Action.

Not simply an interest in the position, but a genuine connection between DREF's mission and the work I've spent my career doing.

To Soror Hunter, with appreciation

Dear Ms. Hunter,

Thank you again for the opportunity to connect and for sharing your vision for DREF's Charitable Engagement team. I left our conversation even more encouraged by the possibility of contributing to an organization whose mission is so closely connected to both my professional experience and my personal commitment to service.

From reviewing scholarship applications for the DPW Foundation, to reviewing complex proposal materials at HES Facilities Management, to developing the engagement and communications strategy for Food For Our Journey, my work has consistently brought me to the intersection of education, community empowerment, relationship building, and meaningful service.

What draws me to DREF is the opportunity to support both sides of that mission. I understand the importance of charitable stewardship, from maintaining accurate records and serving chapters with care to ensuring that applications and contributions are handled responsibly. I also believe deeply in the power of education, research, scholarships, and programs that create opportunities for African American women, their families, and our communities.

As a proud member of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc. and former NPHC president, I know that the strength of our sisterhood is reflected not only in the relationships we build, but in the work we carry forward together.

I consider myself a professional relationship builder, but I am equally proud of what happens behind the scenes. I am organized, dependable, receptive to guidance, and comfortable taking ownership of responsibilities with limited supervision. I learn quickly, welcome feedback, and take pride in turning direction into thoughtful execution.

Soror Hunter, I would genuinely love the opportunity to support you and the DREF team. I recognize that the organization is entering an important period of rebuilding, and I would be honored to contribute wherever I am needed while learning from your leadership.

Our conversation inspired the possibilities outlined here. They are not intended to replace the work already underway, but to demonstrate how I would approach supporting your priorities with care, initiative, and a steadfast commitment to service.

With sisterly appreciation,

Kayla C. White

05 / Celebrating chapter impact

the story.

There is an importance in amplifying the excellent work chapters are already doing. Here is one real initiative, adapted into three distinct communication formats.

Chapter Program Overview

DFREE Academy & Reinvention Series

Federal City Alumnae Chapter is welcoming the public to its 2026–2027 DFREE Academy & Reinvention Series, a six-session financial literacy program designed to strengthen everyday money management and long-term planning.

The program includes four in-person and two virtual sessions, covering saving, investing, money management and preparing for the future. Sessions begin November 12, with a graduation planned during the chapter’s Economic Empowerment Expo in April.

Workshop materials are free of charge. Interested participants are directed to the chapter for application details.
